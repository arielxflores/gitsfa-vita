Source: https://firstassaultonline.fandom.com (about 120 pages, almost all last edited in Aug-Sep 2016, i.e. before v2.0).
Facts only, summarised in our own words. Where the user's v2.0 videos disagree, the videos win.

## Damage model (page "Weapon statistics")
- Hitscan.
- The listed DAMAGE is per bullet on the torso; limbs take 70-90 % depending on the weapon type.
- Headshot multiplier "around 4.3x" (the same page also says the listed value applies to the head; treat as unverified,
  check against time-to-kill in the user's footage before changing ours: we use head = body x 2.29 for rifles).
- RANGE = the distance of full damage; beyond it damage drops off to a minimum of 70 %.
- Pistols and shotguns stop registering hits past some cut-off distance.
- ACCURACY: the first shot is the most accurate; spread grows with each shot.
- STABILITY: less recoil, and a faster return of the spread after firing stops.

## Attachments (v1 market; v2.0 armory shows BASIC parts on every gun)
Laser sights +2/+3 accuracy, -1/-2 move speed; brain connectors shift damage between optimal / effective / extreme range;
long barrels +damage +10 % effective range -5 move; short barrels -damage -10 % range +5/+10 move; flash guards +2 accuracy;
suppressors +2/+4 stability, -3/-5 range, -10/-12 m detection; grips +3 stability -2 move; bipods +2..+7 stability -2/-5 move
-5 swap; drum magazines 50/100, -10 stability; dual magazine +1 magazine, -5 stability.

## Modes
- Team Death Match: 100 points a kill, first to 10,000.
- Demolition: no respawns within a round; sides swap after 5 rounds; first to 6 wins.
- Terminal Conquest: terminals A-E, the teams start near B and D; win by taking all of the other side's terminals or 5 in total;
  a Tachikoma is summoned from a gauge filled by capturing and by hacking / destroying the enemy Tachikoma.
- Ghost Assault: survive a timer against cyborgs on Headquarters or Geofront (unlocked at level 7 then).
